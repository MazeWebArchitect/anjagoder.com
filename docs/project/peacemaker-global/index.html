Peacemaker Global

July 3, 2020
by Anja Goder
in Projects

The Peacemaker Project by Anja Goder is focusing on those  individuals that have acted, and are still fighting, as prominent advocates for peace in the world during the last decades. Through the power of photography, Peacemaker wants to truly capture the very essence of these personalities and honour their lifelong achievements based on determination, dedication and lifelong commitment.
