1,2…Fight!

1,2…Fight!

September 30, 2019
by Anja Goder
in Projects

My first experience with the world of Boxing was around two years ago in Cuba. Back then I totally fell in love with the super-talented Jorge, a little 4-year-old boy who had just started trying out the ring. I had also managed to interview the legendary 3 times Olympic gold medal winner Felix Savon.

Back here in Malta, last Saturday I was invited to a series of professional fights. Although this is not really my usual cup of tea, I was totally impressed with the fairness, the respect between the fighters outside and inside the ring and their absolute determination to fight till the end and win.
