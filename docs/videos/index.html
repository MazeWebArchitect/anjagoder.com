PEACEMAKER VOL. 1 – POPE FRANCIS

What inspired Peacemaker? I am asked so often what inspired the title of the Pope Francis book, and here is the answer: In 2019, Pope Francis signed together with the Grand Imam Ahmad Al-Tayyeb the “Document on Human Fraternity for World Peace and Living Together” in Abu Dhabi. Peacemaker Vol. 1 features the document on its first pages. The Document is a unique declaration banning all kinds of extremism and terrorism and promoting mutual respect, understanding and interreligious dialogue. It is a milestone in Catholic and religious history.

The book with unique pictures of Pope Francis mission is the Perfect Gift for Catholics, and you can buy it here: https://linktr.ee/anjagoder
