Cabrios are a girl’s best friend

Classic cars such as the colourful convertible Cabriole are synonymous with the vibrant society of Cuba. This passion for cars in the Cuban community starts from a very young age, and the joy in children’s faces is easy to see. I have a deep passion to capture children from different societies in moments that show an unspoilt Joy of Life – as can easily be seen by this girl’s blissful expression as she sits in the family car.
