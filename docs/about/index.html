Photography, for me, is more than just capturing moments—it’s about revealing the soul and heart of people, events, and places. Through my lens, I strive to tell stories that resonate deeply, evoking emotions and preserving fleeting instants in time.

My journey in photography has led me to remarkable experiences, including serving as the Head of the Jury and CEO at the Malta International Photo Award (see more on www.maltainternationalphotoaward.com).

I have also had the extraordinary privilege of traveling with Pope Francis through Asia, documenting his mission of peace and unity. This journey culminated in my published work, Peacemaker – Pope Francis on his Mission to Travel to Thailand, Japan, and Bari, which highlights his profound commitment to global dialogue. The book also features the Document on Human Fraternity, a historic declaration signed by Pope Francis and Grand Imam Ahmed el-Tayeb in Abu Dhabi in 2019 (see more on www.whitepagespublishing.com)

Every photograph I take is a testament to the beauty of human connection, the power of moments, and the stories that shape our world.
